Uneton Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Uneton is operated by {{.Operator}}. For privacy questions or data requests, contact {{.Email}}.

What Uneton stores

Uneton stores your Sign in with Apple account identifier, the name you provide at sign-in, family membership and invitation records, device identifiers and notification settings. An Apple refresh credential is encrypted on the server. The app also stores the child nickname and birth date you enter, sleep sessions and their context, growth measurements, temperature readings and notes, prediction settings, and synchronization history needed to keep caregivers' devices consistent. These child records are health-related data and are visible to invited caregivers. The app keeps an offline copy and pending changes on your devices. The Watch can retain one unanswered action, including a temperature note, until the iPhone replies or you remove the Watch app.

Uneton receives APNs and Live Activity tokens when you enable those features. The server may record limited error and security diagnostics when operating the service. It does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, sell data, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. Uneton does not read HealthKit or your address book.

How the data is used

Account and device information allows sign-in, session security, synchronization and notifications. Family and child records provide the shared diary, forecasts and growth charts. Forecasts are estimates based on acknowledged diary records and settings; they are not medical advice. Technical records help keep the service reliable and investigate failures. We process these records to provide the service you request and to protect its security and availability.

Sharing and service providers

People you invite into a family can see and change that family's baby records. The family owner can remove caregivers or revoke unclaimed invitations. A caregiver can leave a family after transferring ownership if they own it. Uneton does not make family data public. Apple processes Sign in with Apple and push notifications. The service runs on a server with an access-controlled SQLite database and a Litestream backup replica. We disclose data to a provider only as needed for these functions or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Account and diary records remain while the account or shared family is active. Deleting a baby or diary item removes it from ordinary views, but its tombstone and synchronization history can remain while the family exists so devices can converge. Expired invitation records can also remain until their family is deleted. Sent notification delivery records are routinely pruned. You can leave a family or, as its sole caregiver, delete it in the app. You can also delete your account in the app. Deletion removes your credentials, devices and family memberships. If another caregiver belongs to a family you own, ownership transfers to that caregiver and the shared family diary remains available to them. A family with no remaining caregiver is deleted with its diary. A deidentified account tombstone may remain for referential integrity. Litestream backup copies rotate on a separate 24-hour schedule and may temporarily contain deleted records. Deletion after a backup restore must be applied again.

You can also ask about access, correction or deletion by emailing {{.Email}}. Removing the app from one device does not delete the shared server account or family data. A device that loses family access can retain an offline cache and unsent changes until sign-out or app removal.

Children and caregivers

Uneton is for caregivers, not for children to use themselves. A caregiver decides what child information to enter and whom to invite. Do not enter a child's full legal name or other details that are not needed for the diary.
